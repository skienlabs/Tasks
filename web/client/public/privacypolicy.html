Tasks

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · updated October 31, 2026 · applies to the Tasks app for Android and the web app at tasks.skienlab.com

The short version
  • We keep what you put into Tasks (tasks, steps, notes, tags, images) so it can sync between your devices. That's all it's used for.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data. Only people you choose to work with in a team see what you share there.
  • You can delete anything at any time, including your whole account.

Tasks is a personal day planner made by Skien Software Lab (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, write to skienlabs@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, and the name and profile photo address from your Google account if you sign in with Google. If you sign up with email and password, you can choose the name yourself.
  • A user ID created by the sign-in service, and when you signed up and last used the app.

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). We never see or store it.

What you put into the app

  • Tasks: title, notes, date, priority, status (open, done, cancelled), how often it was postponed, and when it was created, changed or completed.
  • Steps (sub-tasks) and whether each one is ticked.
  • Tags: name, colour and an optional logo image you choose.
  • Time limits you set on tasks (a date and time).
  • Teams, if you use them: the team's name and colour, who is in it and their role (owner, admin, member), the email addresses invited and who invited them, and which tasks were given to whom and by whom; comments, files, job titles, groups, team tags, templates, repeating tasks, join requests, the activity log and notifications.
  • Images you attach to tasks. Before upload, the app shrinks each picture and saves it again as a new image file. That step drops the photo's hidden details (EXIF data), such as where it was taken.

Technical data

  • Our server logs each request (time, the address requested, the result, and your device's IP address) to keep the service running and fix problems. These logs aren't used to profile you, and they're deleted regularly.

What we don't collect

  • No location, contacts, call logs, SMS, microphone, or device identifiers for advertising.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no advertising networks.
  • The app only ever sees photos you choose yourself or take with the camera button. It can't browse your gallery.

How we use it

  • To show your tasks on every device you sign in on, and keep them in sync.
  • To work out your progress: the daily counts, the monthly summary, the end-of-day notification and the home-screen widgets.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't build profiles of you, and nobody reads your tasks.

On your phone

  • Saved copy: the Android app and the web app keep a copy of your tasks, tags and images on your device (the web app in your browser's storage), together with changes made offline that haven't been sent yet, so they open fast and work offline. This copy is removed when you sign out (or uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser).
  • End-of-day notification: worked out on your phone from your own tasks. You can switch it off or change its time in the app (Account), or in Android settings.
  • Widgets show your tasks on your home screen. Anyone who can see your unlocked home screen can see them, so only add them if you're happy with that.

Teams: what teammates see

  • Everyone in a team sees its name, and the name, email address, profile photo and role of the other people in it.
  • A team's owner and admins see the email addresses invited to it, and the tasks the team gave out: their title, notes, steps, images, day, time limit and status (who completed what, and when). They don't see your own tasks, or the tags you put on a team task.
  • When you invite someone, we keep their email address so the invite can wait for them in the app. We don't email them; you send them the message yourself. They can decline, and an admin can cancel the invite.
  • Comments, files and images on a team task are seen by its person, whoever gave it, the others it was given to, and the team's admins. Your job title (if set) is seen by everyone in the team.
  • Admins see the team's activity log (who gave, finished, approved or commented on which task, and when) and reports built from the team's tasks, which they can export as CSV or PDF.
  • If you open a team's invite link and ask to join, its admins see your name and email address with the request.
  • Notifications (team news) are kept for 90 days.
  • If you leave a team, are removed, or the team is deleted, the tasks it gave you stay yours and the team's admins no longer see them.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or trade your data, and we don't share it for advertising. Apart from teammates (see above), only these services handle it, solely to run Tasks:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: signs you in. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Cloud: hosts our server, where your data is stored.

We would only disclose data if the law required it, and only as much as required.

Security

  • All traffic between the apps and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every request must carry a valid sign-in token, and each account can only reach its own data. Images are only served to their owner.
  • The server runs on a private machine that only we can access.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your data.

How long we keep it

  • Your tasks, tags and images stay for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete a task, it's removed straight away. Its images are kept for 24 hours so “Undo” can restore them, then deleted.
  • When your account is deleted, all of its data is deleted with it (see below).

Delete your data or your account

You can delete any task, step, image or tag in the app at any time.

To delete your account and everything in it, email skienlabs@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Tasks account”. Within 30 days we will delete:
  • your account and its sign-in,
  • all your tasks, steps, tags, logos and images.
Then we'll confirm by email. Server logs are deleted on their usual schedule.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the DPDP Act in India), you may have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you, or get a copy of it,
  • have it corrected or deleted,
  • object to how it's used,
  • complain to your data protection authority.

Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. We process your data because it's needed to provide the service you signed up for.

Children

Tasks is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
